About VoxGrid

Where civic voices
shape real decisions.

VoxGrid is a civic engagement platform that connects citizens with the local governance issues that matter most — enabling informed debate, community polling, and transparent participation in democracy.

What We Do

A modern record of civic life

VoxGrid tracks local government issues, policy debates, and civic decisions in real time. Whether it's city council term limits, housing policy, or environmental initiatives — VoxGrid surfaces what's happening in your community and gives you the tools to engage.

We believe every citizen deserves access to clear, contextualised information about the decisions that shape their lives. VoxGrid bridges the gap between institutions and the people they serve.

  • Issue Feed Browse and follow live civic issues categorised by topic — governance, housing, environment, transport, and more.
  • Community Polls Vote on pressing civic questions and see how your community feels about critical policy matters in real time.
  • Discussions Join structured conversations on each issue — share perspectives, cite evidence, and engage constructively with fellow citizens.
  • Issue Archive Track how civic issues evolve over time with a full history of debates, poll results, and policy outcomes.
  • Join Conversations Add your voice to live debates and help shape the community's understanding of complex local decisions.
  • Civic Partners & Research Access reports and analysis from vetted civic organisations, academics, and local journalism partners.

User Guidelines & Social Responsibility

VoxGrid is a public civic space. To keep it trustworthy and valuable for every citizen, all members are expected to uphold these principles.

Be Honest & Factual

Share information you believe to be true and cite your sources when possible. Do not spread misinformation, fabricated data, or deliberately misleading claims about civic issues or public figures.

Respect All Perspectives

Civic discourse thrives on diverse viewpoints. Engage with opposing views respectfully and in good faith — debate ideas, not people. Personal attacks, harassment, and intimidation are not tolerated.

Stay Non-Partisan

VoxGrid is not affiliated with any political party or ideology. Avoid content that promotes party propaganda. Focus on policies, evidence, and community outcomes rather than partisan loyalty.

Protect Community Privacy

Do not share other users' personal information without consent. Respect the anonymity of community members and avoid publishing identifying details about private individuals.

No Hate Speech or Discrimination

Content that demeans, dehumanises, or discriminates on the basis of race, religion, gender, caste, disability, or sexual orientation will be removed and may result in account suspension.

No Manipulation or Spam

Do not use bots, multiple accounts, or coordinated campaigns to artificially inflate votes or suppress opposing viewpoints. Authentic participation only — the integrity of civic data depends on it.

Report, Don't Retaliate

If you see content that violates these guidelines, use the report feature rather than engaging in counter-attacks. Our moderation team reviews all reports and acts within 48 hours.

Think Before You Post

Civic conversations can influence real policy decisions. Consider the impact of your words on your community, especially when discussing sensitive local issues affecting vulnerable groups.
